How to play Jigsaw Puzzles?
Choose a beautiful puzzle from the gallery, pick your difficulty level (number of pieces), and tap Play! Drag and drop puzzle pieces to assemble them. Pieces that belong together will snap automatically.
How to restore my Premium subscription?
If you have previously purchased the Premium version, go to Settings and tap "Restore". Make sure you are logged into the same Apple Account used for the purchase.
What benefits does Premium offer?
Premium status completely removes interstitial and banner ads, unlocks special premium puzzle categories, grants unlimited hints, and offers multiplied daily rewards!
