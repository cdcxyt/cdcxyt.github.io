🧩 How do I play Jigsaw Cards?
Slide and arrange the puzzle cards freely across the wooden board. When cards that belong next to each other in the original artwork meet side by side, they will smoothly snap together into a larger united block! Continue connecting matching pieces until the complete masterpiece is revealed.
🎴 What are the face-down cards?
In some of the trickier puzzles, certain cards start with their decorative backs facing up! Simply tap on any face-down card to take a quick peek at its hidden picture. Memorize the pattern, slide it to connect with its neighbor, and it will stay face up forever once merged!
💡 How do Hints work when I get stuck?
Whenever you need a gentle nudge, tap the glowing Hint lightbulb in the bottom right corner. It will show you exactly which card to move and guide it to its perfect spot. You start with complimentary hints, and you can easily collect 3 extra hints whenever you wish by watching a short video. Free daily hints are also gifted to you every day!
✈️ Can I play offline without Wi-Fi?
Yes, absolutely! Your puzzles and gallery achievements are stored directly on your iPhone and iPad. You can enjoy quiet, cozy puzzle sessions anytime, anywhere—whether you are on a flight, traveling outdoors, or enjoying a calm morning without an internet connection.
